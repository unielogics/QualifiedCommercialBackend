1 Definitions

1.1  Actual Funding

“Actual Funding” means the irrevocable disbursement and clearance of at least the Minimum Activation Amount:

  1. Into an account owned or controlled by the Dealer;
  2. Directly to an approved creditor, seller, floorplan provider, inventory source, or other approved payee on behalf of the Dealer; or
  3. Into an approved controlled account for the benefit of the Dealer.

Actual Funding does not include a prequalification; an underwriting indication; a term sheet; a letter of interest; a commitment subject to unsatisfied conditions; an approval that has not funded; an unadvanced credit limit; a failed or reversed transfer; a test deposit; or an oral or written promise of future funding.

1.2  Activation Agreement

“Activation Agreement” means the separate Program Activation and Production Agreement executed at or after closing, which contains the Funding Activation Certificate, the exact production and remittance thresholds, and the operative reporting, default, and remedy provisions. This Agreement does not set those thresholds and does not itself activate them.

1.3  Activation Date

“Activation Date” means the date stated in the Funding Activation Certificate delivered with the Activation Agreement, which may not be earlier than the date Actual Funding occurs. It is the date on which the Closing Package supersedes this Agreement as provided in Section 4.8.

1.4  Application Documents

“Application Documents” means the separate Qualified Commercial application, credit authorization, bank-verification authorization, document-sharing authorization, privacy notice, electronic-signature consent, and related application materials used before funding. Application Documents are separate from this Agreement.

1.5  Business-Purpose Financing

“Business-Purpose Financing” means financing whose proceeds are intended primarily for commercial, business, investment, inventory, real estate, equipment, refinancing, working-capital, or other non-personal purposes. It does not include consumer-purpose financing for an individual vehicle buyer.

1.6  Closing Package

“Closing Package” means, collectively, the Funding Documents; the Activation Agreement; the Activation Agreement’s completed Addendum A containing the exact production, penetration, gross-production, remittance, and reporting figures; the executed Funding Activation Certificate; and the completed schedules delivered with them at closing.

1.7  Commitment Date

“Commitment Date” means the Effective Date of this Agreement, which is the date of last signature.

1.8  Covered Products

“Covered Products” means only the products specifically identified in Schedule A that the Sponsor is legally authorized and operationally able to provide, administer, or support in the applicable jurisdiction. Covered Products may include vehicle service contracts; GAP products; anti-theft products; appearance-protection products; key-replacement products; tire-and-wheel products; maintenance products; powertrain products; ancillary protection products; and other approved F&I or aftermarket products.

1.9  Dealer Capital Relationship Manager

“Dealer Capital Relationship Manager” or “Relationship Manager” means the authorized Qualified Commercial or Sponsor representative identified in Schedule B. The Relationship Manager is not personally a lender, creditor, bank representative, fiduciary, guarantor, attorney, accountant, investment adviser, or consumer mortgage loan originator merely because the representative performs services contemplated by this Agreement.

1.10  Dealer Customer Information

“Dealer Customer Information” means any nonpublic personal, financial, credit, transaction, financing, or identifying information concerning an individual vehicle buyer, applicant, lessee, guarantor, or customer.

1.11  Exclusivity Window

“Exclusivity Window” means the period beginning on the date Qualified Commercial issues a Written Approval and continuing for the number of days stated in Schedule A, during which the Dealer will pursue the approved financing exclusively through Qualified Commercial as provided in Section 3. The Exclusivity Window ends automatically on the earliest of its stated expiration; Actual Funding; the Outside Funding Date; written withdrawal of the Written Approval by Qualified Commercial; or written release by Qualified Commercial.

2 Conditional Effectiveness and Scope

2.1  What This Agreement Does

This Agreement governs the commitment stage only. It records the Dealer’s voluntary election to participate in the integrated program; the Dealer’s conditional commitment to route Covered Product production if the contemplated financing funds; the exclusivity arrangement in Section 3; the application, information, and compensation-disclosure framework; and the roles of the Parties before closing.

It does not close a loan, set production numbers, or create a payment obligation. Those are addressed exclusively in the Activation Agreement.

2.2  No Pre-Funding Dealer Payment or Production Obligation

Before Actual Funding:

  1. The Dealer owes no fee under this Agreement;
  2. The Dealer has no obligation to route, produce, or remit any Covered Product;
  3. No production, penetration, remittance, or reporting covenant applies;
  4. No default rate or pricing adjustment applies;
  5. No non-circumvention restriction applies, except the Exclusivity Window in Section 3;
  6. Qualified Commercial and the Sponsor have no obligation to fund the Dealer;
  7. The Relationship Manager has no personal claim against the Dealer for time devoted to the application; and
  8. No Party may claim lost profits, lost production, expected commissions, reliance damages, or expectancy damages based solely on the failure of financing to close.

2.3  Separate Pre-Funding Authorizations

The Dealer’s application, credit authorization, bank-verification authorization, document-sharing consent, confidentiality obligations, and electronic-signature consent are governed by the separately executed Application Documents. This Agreement does not replace those documents.

2.4  Conditions to Moving to the Activation Agreement

The Parties will proceed to the Activation Agreement only after:

  1. The Dealer and Funding Party execute the final Funding Documents;
  2. Actual Funding of at least the Minimum Activation Amount occurs;
  3. The Activation Agreement’s Addendum A has been completed with exact production and remittance thresholds;
  4. Schedule C of this Agreement has been completed and delivered to the Dealer;
  5. The Sponsor confirms that it is authorized and operationally capable of administering the Covered Products;
  6. Any legally required state-specific commercial-financing disclosure has been delivered;
  7. Any required licenses, registrations, approvals, or exemptions have been confirmed; and
  8. No applicable Funding Party, regulator, or counsel has prohibited the contemplated product or compensation structure.

No funds should be released until the Funding Activation Certificate in the Activation Agreement is included in the closing package.

2.5  No Activation Through Approval Alone

A Written Approval, term sheet, letter of interest, conditional commitment, credit memorandum, lender discussion, or undrawn credit line does not create a production obligation and does not activate the Activation Agreement.

2.6  Outside Funding Date

If Actual Funding has not occurred by the Outside Funding Date, this Agreement automatically expires without fee; exclusivity; production obligation; termination payment; or continuing liability. The Parties may extend the Outside Funding Date only through a signed written amendment.

3 Exclusivity Window

3.1  What the Dealer Commits To

The Dealer may decline the program, seek financing elsewhere, or withdraw its application at any time before Qualified Commercial issues a Written Approval, without obligation.

Once Qualified Commercial issues a Written Approval, the Dealer agrees that during the Exclusivity Window it will pursue the approved financing exclusively through Qualified Commercial, and will not submit the same financing request to another broker, intermediary, or capital source for the purpose of closing that request elsewhere.

3.2  What the Exclusivity Window Does Not Do

The Exclusivity Window does not:

  1. Obligate the Dealer to accept the approved financing;
  2. Obligate the Dealer to sign the Funding Documents;
  3. Create any fee if the Dealer declines the financing;
  4. Create any production or remittance obligation;
  5. Restrict ordinary trade credit, routine inventory purchases, or an existing disclosed floorplan arrangement;
  6. Restrict a preexisting relationship disclosed in Schedule D;
  7. Restrict emergency financing reasonably needed to prevent immediate material harm to the Dealer’s business;
  8. Restrict consumer financing for an individual vehicle buyer; or
  9. Restrict financing unrelated to the approved request.

3.3  Dealer Right to Exit

The Dealer may end the Exclusivity Window at any time by written notice stating that it is declining the approved financing. On delivery of that notice the Dealer owes no fee and no further obligation under this Section, and Qualified Commercial will not treat the Dealer’s subsequent financing activity as a breach.

3.4  Qualified Commercial’s Reciprocal Obligation

During the Exclusivity Window, Qualified Commercial will pursue the approved financing diligently and in good faith; keep the Dealer reasonably informed of file status; promptly identify outstanding conditions; and notify the Dealer in writing if it determines the financing is no longer achievable. If Qualified Commercial fails to pursue the file for fifteen consecutive business days without a documented reason, the Exclusivity Window ends automatically.

3.5  Remedy for Breach

A proven intentional breach of Section 3.1 may entitle Qualified Commercial to its actual, documented out-of-pocket costs incurred in pursuing the approved financing, and to reasonable enforcement costs awarded by a court. Speculative future production, lost expected compensation, punitive damages, and undisclosed liquidated damages are not recoverable under this Section.

4 Conditional Production Commitment

4.1  The Commitment

Conditioned on and effective only upon Actual Funding, the Dealer commits that during the Program Term it will route 100% of the Covered Products actually sold in eligible retail transactions through the Sponsor Platform, except for an approved exclusion.

This commitment is the consideration the Dealer offers in exchange for Sponsor participation, Qualified Commercial’s continuing services, program administration, reporting and monitoring, product economics, and any Program Support identified in Schedule A. It becomes operative and enforceable only through the Activation Agreement.

4.2  Products Covered

The commitment applies only to the Covered Products identified in Schedule A that the Sponsor is legally authorized and operationally able to provide in the applicable jurisdiction. A product is not subject to the commitment when the Sponsor is not legally authorized to provide it; does not offer it; cannot timely process it; rejects an otherwise eligible contract without reasonable grounds; a regulator prohibits it; or the Parties approve an exception in writing.

4.3  Thresholds Are Set at Closing

No production, penetration, gross-production, remittance, or reporting number is fixed by this Agreement. Those numbers are established from the verified baseline in Schedule E and stated as exact operative figures in the Activation Agreement’s Addendum A, delivered as part of the Closing Package. The completed Addendum A in the Closing Package is the sole source of those figures. No threshold may be enforced against the Dealer unless it appears there as an exact figure.

4.4  Compliant Offer Standard

The commitment requires only that the Dealer make Covered Products reasonably available for compliant presentation by properly trained personnel. It does not require a customer to buy a product; misleading bundling; a hidden charge; a false representation that a product is mandatory; the falsification of a customer election; improper packing of payments; or conduct prohibited by law.

4.5  No Customer Tying

No vehicle buyer may be required to purchase a Covered Product as a condition of buying a vehicle; receiving an advertised vehicle price; receiving consumer financing; receiving a quoted interest rate; receiving another product; receiving a rebate; or completing a transaction, unless a specific arrangement is lawful, accurately disclosed, and approved by qualified counsel.

4.6  Baseline Verification

Before closing, the Parties will complete Schedule E using DMS records, Sponsor reports, bank records, product reports, or other reliable evidence. The Dealer will not be asked to accept an operative threshold that exceeds its verified baseline performance except by its own written agreement.

4.7  Where the Final Numbers Are Set

The final operative numbers for this program will be set in the Closing Package and nowhere else. The completed Addendum A delivered in the Closing Package is the sole source of the production, penetration, gross-production, remittance, and reporting figures.

The Dealer is entitled to review the completed Addendum A before executing the Activation Agreement.

4.8  Closing Package Supersedes This Agreement

On the Activation Date, the Closing Package supersedes this Agreement as to the production, penetration, gross-production, remittance, reporting, monitoring, audit, default, remedy, pricing-adjustment, term, and termination terms of the program. From that date forward those terms are governed exclusively by the Closing Package.

Where a figure, date, threshold, or term appears both in this Agreement and in the Closing Package, the Closing Package controls. No Party may rely on this Agreement to enforce a production, remittance, or reporting obligation against the Dealer after the Activation Date, or to assert a figure that differs from the completed Addendum A.

After the Activation Date this Agreement survives only as to: the expiration of the Exclusivity Window under Section 3; the compensation and conflict disclosures in Schedule C; the relationship disclosures in Schedule D; the verified baseline in Schedule E as the record supporting the figures adopted at closing; and Sections 13, 14, 15, 16, and 17 to the extent they expressly survive.

4.9  If No Closing Package Is Executed

If Actual Funding does not occur, or the Parties do not execute the Activation Agreement, no production obligation ever takes effect and this Agreement expires as provided in Section 2.6. The Dealer’s conditional commitment under this Section 4 does not survive that expiration.

5 Separate Funding Documents and Party Roles

5.1  Qualified Commercial’s Capacity

Unless expressly stated otherwise in the Funding Documents, Qualified Commercial acts as program manager; business-purpose capital advisor; application and documentation coordinator; funding arranger; reporting coordinator; relationship-management platform; monitoring provider; and Sponsor-program coordinator. Qualified Commercial does not become the lender merely by receiving, compiling, analyzing, presenting, or transmitting financing information.

5.2  Funding Party’s Independent Decision

The Funding Party has exclusive authority over final credit approval; loan amount; interest rate; fees; amortization; term; collateral; guaranties; conditions precedent; adverse-action decisions; funding; servicing; default; acceleration; and enforcement of the Funding Documents. Neither the Sponsor nor the Relationship Manager may override a Funding Party decision.

5.3  No Oral Lending Commitment

No oral representation by Qualified Commercial, the Sponsor, or the Relationship Manager binds the Funding Party. Only a final written instrument issued or signed by the Funding Party may constitute a binding financing commitment.

5.4  Order of Precedence

In the event of a direct conflict:

  1. The Funding Documents control principal, interest, payment, collateral, guaranty, default, and lender remedies;
  2. The Activation Agreement controls whether and when the program activated;
  3. The Activation Agreement controls production and remittance thresholds;
  4. Schedule C controls disclosed compensation; and
  5. This Agreement controls the commitment-stage election, exclusivity, application, and disclosure obligations, and is superseded as provided in Section 4.8 once the Closing Package is executed.

No Funding Document may silently expand the Relationship Manager’s compensation.

5.5  Optional Funding Party Joinder

A Funding Party may become a Party or express third-party beneficiary only through a written joinder identifying the provisions it may enforce.

6 Dealer Capital Relationship Manager

6.1  Designation

The Relationship Manager assigned to the Dealer is identified in Schedule B. The Relationship Manager is an employee or authorized contractor of Qualified Commercial or the Sponsor and is not an employee, agent, partner, or fiduciary of the Dealer or Funding Party.

6.2  Acknowledgment of Pre-Funding Time

The Dealer acknowledges that the Relationship Manager may devote substantial pre-funding time to visiting the dealership; understanding the Dealer’s business; explaining the program; gathering authorized business information; identifying missing documents; coordinating owner authorizations; scheduling review calls; assisting with use-of-funds detail; organizing financial information; and preparing the Dealer for underwriting. That time is devoted at the risk of the Relationship Manager’s employer and creates no Dealer payment obligation if Actual Funding does not occur.

6.3  Permitted Pre-Funding Activities

Subject to applicable law and company policy, the Relationship Manager may:

  1. Provide general information concerning available business-purpose programs;
  2. Help the Dealer navigate the application workflow;
  3. Collect documents with the Dealer’s authorization;
  4. Explain why information is requested;
  5. Coordinate communications;
  6. Relay written questions between the Dealer and underwriting;
  7. Assist the Dealer in describing its intended use of funds;
  8. Explain nonbinding program requirements;
  9. Help the Dealer prepare financial schedules; and
  10. Schedule qualified personnel to discuss specific financing terms.

6.4  Prohibited Activities

The Relationship Manager may not:

  1. Promise approval, pricing, an amount, or a closing date;
  2. Make or override a credit decision;
  3. Represent that the Relationship Manager speaks for a bank unless the bank has expressly authorized that representation;
  4. Alter a lender’s written terms;
  5. Sign, initial, draw, adopt, or apply the Dealer’s signature;
  6. Complete an owner’s credit authorization for that owner;
  7. Obtain a consumer report without a permissible purpose and proper authorization;
  8. Display or disclose a raw consumer credit score to unauthorized personnel;
  9. Collect undisclosed cash, checks, application fees, or closing fees;
  10. Steer the Dealer toward more expensive financing to increase compensation;
  11. Hold themselves out as an attorney, accountant, lender, or fiduciary;
  12. Arrange consumer auto financing for an individual vehicle buyer under this Agreement;
  13. Store Social Security numbers, raw credit reports, or customer financial information on an unauthorized device;
  14. Require a vehicle buyer to purchase a Covered Product; or
  15. Make a statement inconsistent with the final written Funding Documents.

6.5  Post-Funding Role

After activation, the Relationship Manager will serve as the Dealer’s primary program contact and will coordinate the Capital Health Services provided under the Activation Agreement.

6.6  No Personal Liability

The Relationship Manager does not personally guarantee funding; program performance; Sponsor performance; product claims; Dealer performance; repayment; or any Funding Party obligation.

6.7  Replacement

Qualified Commercial or the Sponsor may replace the Relationship Manager upon notice to the Dealer. Replacement does not terminate this Agreement, provided the replacement is appropriately trained and assumes the continuing responsibilities in Schedule B.

7 Compensation and Conflict Disclosure

7.1  No Bank Points or Bank Commission to Relationship Manager

The Relationship Manager will receive no bank points; no lender-paid closing commission; no percentage of the Funding Facility; no portion of the interest rate; no lender spread; no portion of a default rate; no percentage of lender fees; and no compensation from the Funding Party for directing the Dealer to that Funding Party.

7.2  Permitted Internal Compensation

Qualified Commercial or the Sponsor may compensate the Relationship Manager under a separate written compensation arrangement using one or more of the following: salary; hourly compensation; fixed retainer; fixed implementation compensation based on documented work; fixed recurring account-management compensation; compensation for ongoing Dealer support; lawful and disclosed compensation attributable to actual Covered Product sales or servicing; or other compensation not constituting Restricted Compensation.

7.3  No Disguised Closing Commission

A payment will not become compliant merely because it is labeled an implementation fee, consulting fee, marketing fee, or service fee. Compensation must bear a reasonable relationship to documented services actually performed and may not be calculated from approval; principal amount; interest rate; bank fees; lender yield; default pricing; or the Dealer’s acceptance of a more costly financing option.

7.4  Commencement of Account-Management Compensation

A fixed post-funding account-management payment may begin after activation because ongoing account-management services begin at that time. Such payment must compensate continuing services and must not be a payment for the mere fact that a loan closed.

7.5  No Dealer Fee Before Funding

The Dealer owes no application, consulting, intermediary, advisory, packaging, commitment, or success fee under this Agreement before Actual Funding. Third-party costs ordered with the Dealer’s prior written authorization may be governed by a separate written authorization, but may not be mischaracterized as a Relationship Manager commission.

7.6  Dealer-Paid Compensation

The Dealer owes no compensation to the Relationship Manager personally. Any post-funding fee payable by the Dealer to Qualified Commercial or the Sponsor must be:

  1. Identified in Schedule C;
  2. Stated as a dollar amount or objective formula;
  3. Described by purpose;
  4. Separately disclosed from Funding Party charges;
  5. Lawful in the applicable jurisdiction; and
  6. Accepted by an authorized Dealer representative.

If Schedule C states “$0,” no Dealer-paid fee is owed.

7.7  Sponsor and Program Economics

Qualified Commercial and the Sponsor may receive disclosed compensation from product administration; platform services; monitoring; reporting; servicing; technology; program management; account management; lawful product-sales economics; or other services identified in Schedule C. No Party may conceal compensation inside a product charge, loan amount, interest rate, or closing statement.

7.8  Compensation Disclosure at Closing

Schedule C must disclose every Dealer-paid fee; every Funding Party-paid fee to Qualified Commercial or the Sponsor; every recurring program fee; whether compensation is included in the cost of financing; the general source of Sponsor product economics; the Relationship Manager’s compensation category; any potential conflict of interest; and whether an applicable state or federal compensation form is required.

7.9  SBA Financing

Any SBA 7(a), SBA 504, or other SBA-supported transaction is subject to separate SBA requirements. Where applicable:

  1. Required SBA compensation forms must be completed;
  2. Agent, packager, referral, and lender-service-provider roles must be accurately identified;
  3. Compensation must be reasonable and disclosed;
  4. Prohibited dual compensation must not occur;
  5. This Agreement does not replace SBA Form 159 or another required SBA document; and
  6. No person may rely on this Agreement to avoid SBA compensation rules.

7.10  Licensing and State Requirements

A Relationship Manager or Party may perform only those activities permitted by applicable law. If a state requires a commercial-financing broker license, registration, disclosure, bond, appointment, or other authorization: the affected activity must be suspended until compliance is confirmed; a state-specific addendum may be required; no compensation may be collected in violation of that state’s law; and the Parties will not rely solely on the absence of bank-paid compensation as proof that no broker regulation applies.

8 Voluntary Program Election and Anti-Tying Safeguards

8.1  Voluntary Election Before Funding

Before Qualified Commercial issues a Written Approval, the Dealer may decline the integrated program; seek financing elsewhere; decline Sponsor products; withdraw its application; and decline to sign the Funding Documents, without incurring an obligation under this Agreement. After a Written Approval is issued, the same rights continue except that the Dealer’s pursuit of the approved financing through another intermediary is subject to the Exclusivity Window in Section 3, which the Dealer may end at any time by declining the approved financing under Section 3.3.

8.2  Separate Sponsor-Supported Program

The Dealer acknowledges that, after Actual Funding, the product-production and reporting commitments it will assume under the Activation Agreement constitute consideration for Sponsor participation; Qualified Commercial’s continuing services; program administration; reporting and monitoring; product economics; any identified Program Support; and the integrated structure voluntarily selected by the Dealer.

8.3  No Bank Product Requirement Representation

No Party may represent that a bank requires the Dealer to purchase or use a Sponsor product unless:

  1. The requirement is accurately stated in the Funding Party’s written documents;
  2. The Funding Party has expressly approved the statement;
  3. The Funding Party’s counsel has confirmed the structure; and
  4. The requirement is lawful.

8.4  Independent Credit Underwriting

The Funding Party’s credit decision should be based on independently evaluated factors such as cash flow; DSCR; EBITDA or operating income; bank activity; repayment capacity; Dealer history; product-production history; collateral; guarantees; controlled cash flow; existing debt; chargeback performance; and other legitimate underwriting considerations.

8.5  Compliance Modification

If the Funding Party, a regulator, or qualified counsel determines that a product, exclusivity, pricing, or remittance provision creates an impermissible tying concern, the Parties will modify, sever, or waive the affected provision to the minimum extent necessary. Such modification will not excuse the Dealer’s payment obligations under the Funding Documents.

8.6  No Customer Tying

No vehicle buyer may be required to purchase a Covered Product as a condition of buying a vehicle; receiving an advertised vehicle price; receiving consumer financing; receiving a quoted interest rate; receiving another product; receiving a rebate; or completing a transaction, unless a specific arrangement is lawful, accurately disclosed, and approved by qualified counsel.

  2. Maintain appropriate safeguards;
  3. Limit subcontractor access;
  4. Contractually require appropriate subcontractor protections;
  5. Cooperate with reasonable security assessments;
  6. Notify the Dealer of a material security incident;
  7. Assist with legally required response and notification; and
  8. Return or securely dispose of information when no longer required.

10.5  Security Incident Notice

A Party discovering unauthorized access to protected information will notify affected Parties without unreasonable delay and, contractually, within 24 hours after confirming a material incident, unless prohibited by law or law enforcement. The notice will provide available information concerning the nature of the incident; information affected; systems involved; containment; remediation; required notices; and ongoing risk.
