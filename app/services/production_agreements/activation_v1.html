1 Definitions

1.1  Actual Funding

“Actual Funding” means the irrevocable disbursement and clearance of at least the Minimum Activation Amount:

  1. Into an account owned or controlled by the Dealer;
  2. Directly to an approved creditor, seller, floorplan provider, inventory source, or other approved payee on behalf of the Dealer; or
  3. Into an approved controlled account for the benefit of the Dealer.

Actual Funding does not include a prequalification; an underwriting indication; a term sheet; a letter of interest; a commitment subject to unsatisfied conditions; an approval that has not funded; an unadvanced credit limit; a failed or reversed transfer; a test deposit; or an oral or written promise of future funding.

1.2  Activation Certificate

“Activation Certificate” means the closing certificate attached as Schedule 5 identifying the Funding Party, actual amount disbursed, Activation Date, Funding Documents, production commencement date, payment obligation, and other closing information.

1.3  Activation Date

“Activation Date” means the date stated in a properly completed Activation Certificate, which may not be earlier than the date Actual Funding occurs.

1.4  Application Documents

“Application Documents” means the separate Qualified Commercial application, credit authorization, bank-verification authorization, document-sharing authorization, privacy notice, electronic-signature consent, and related application materials used before funding. Application Documents are separate from this Agreement.

1.5  Business-Purpose Financing

“Business-Purpose Financing” means financing whose proceeds are intended primarily for commercial, business, investment, inventory, real estate, equipment, refinancing, working-capital, or other non-personal purposes. It does not include consumer-purpose financing for an individual vehicle buyer.

1.6  Capital Health Services

“Capital Health Services” means the ongoing business-purpose financial-readiness and capital-advisory services described in Section 10.

1.7  Closing Package

“Closing Package” means, collectively, the Funding Documents; this Agreement; the completed Addendum A; the executed Funding Activation Certificate in Schedule 5; and the completed schedules delivered with them at closing. The completed Addendum A is the sole source of the operative production and remittance figures for the program.

1.8  Commitment Agreement

“Commitment Agreement” means the Production Commitment and Capital Engagement Agreement previously executed by the Parties at the approval stage, which recorded the Dealer’s voluntary program election, its conditional production commitment, the Exclusivity Window, and the verified production baseline. As provided in Section 4.8 of the Commitment Agreement, this Agreement and the balance of the Closing Package supersede the Commitment Agreement as to the production, penetration, gross-production, remittance, reporting, monitoring, audit, default, remedy, pricing-adjustment, term, and termination terms of the program, and convert the Dealer’s conditional commitment into the operative obligations set out below. Where a figure or term appears in both, this Agreement controls.

2 Activation and Effectiveness

2.1  This Agreement Is the Closing Instrument

This Agreement is executed at or after closing. It certifies that Actual Funding occurred, fixes the exact production and remittance thresholds, and converts the Dealer’s conditional commitment under the Commitment Agreement into operative obligations.

2.2  Conditions Satisfied

This Agreement takes effect on the Activation Date only after:

  1. The Dealer and Funding Party executed the final Funding Documents;
  2. Actual Funding of at least the Minimum Activation Amount occurred;
  3. The Parties completed and signed the Funding Activation Certificate in Schedule 5;
  4. Addendum A was completed with exact production and remittance thresholds;
  5. Schedule 3 was completed and delivered to the Dealer;
  6. The Sponsor confirmed that it is authorized and operationally capable of administering the Covered Products;
  7. Any legally required state-specific commercial-financing disclosure was delivered; and
  8. Any required licenses, registrations, approvals, or exemptions were confirmed.

No funds should be released until the completed Activation Certificate is included in the closing package.

2.3  No Retroactive Production Obligation

Production occurring before the Production Commencement Date stated in the Activation Certificate will not be used to declare a default unless the Dealer intentionally diverted post-closing production during an agreed implementation period.

2.4  Staged Funding

If the Funding Facility is funded in stages:

  1. The first disbursement must equal or exceed the Minimum Activation Amount;
  2. The Activation Certificate must identify the funded and unfunded portions;
  3. Production and remittance thresholds must be based on actual funded exposure unless otherwise expressly approved;
  4. Undrawn amounts do not create Dealer obligations beyond those tied to funded exposure; and
  5. Holdbacks and future advances remain subject to the Funding Documents.

2.5  Thresholds Must Be Exact

No production, penetration, gross-production, remittance, or reporting threshold may be enforced against the Dealer unless the relevant verified baseline and the exact operative figure appear in a completed Addendum A. A blank field is not enforceable.

2.6  Relationship to the Commitment Agreement

The Exclusivity Window under the Commitment Agreement ended on the occurrence of Actual Funding. The Dealer’s pre-funding protections under that agreement are not revived by this Agreement, and the obligations in this Agreement apply prospectively from the Activation Date. This Agreement, together with the balance of the Closing Package, supersedes the Commitment Agreement to the extent stated in Section 1.8.

3 Separate Funding Documents and Party Roles

3.1  Qualified Commercial’s Capacity

Unless expressly stated otherwise in the Funding Documents, Qualified Commercial acts as program manager; business-purpose capital advisor; application and documentation coordinator; funding arranger; reporting coordinator; relationship-management platform; monitoring provider; and Sponsor-program coordinator. Qualified Commercial does not become the lender merely by receiving, compiling, analyzing, presenting, or transmitting financing information.

3.2  Funding Party’s Independent Decision

The Funding Party has exclusive authority over final credit approval; loan amount; interest rate; fees; amortization; term; collateral; guaranties; conditions precedent; adverse-action decisions; funding; servicing; default; acceleration; and enforcement of the Funding Documents. Neither the Sponsor nor the Relationship Manager may override a Funding Party decision.

3.3  No Oral Lending Commitment

No oral representation by Qualified Commercial, the Sponsor, or the Relationship Manager binds the Funding Party. Only a final written instrument issued or signed by the Funding Party may constitute a binding financing commitment.

3.4  Order of Precedence

In the event of a direct conflict:

  1. The Funding Documents control principal, interest, payment, collateral, guaranty, default, and lender remedies;
  2. The Activation Certificate controls whether and when this Agreement activated;
  3. Addendum A controls production and remittance thresholds;
  4. Schedule 3 controls disclosed compensation; and
  5. This Agreement controls the continuing program, advisory, reporting, product, and relationship obligations.

No Funding Document may silently expand the Relationship Manager’s compensation.

3.5  Optional Funding Party Joinder

A Funding Party may become a Party or express third-party beneficiary only through a written joinder identifying the provisions it may enforce.

The Funding Facility may be used only for the approved purposes stated in Schedule 1 and the Funding Documents. Approved purposes may include inventory acquisition; refinancing identified business debt; dealership working capital; equipment; real estate; renovation; F&I program implementation; expansion; acquisition; approved operating liquidity; or other specifically approved business purposes.

The Dealer will maintain invoices, payoff statements, purchase orders, settlement statements, bank records, and other reasonable evidence showing how proceeds were used.

5 Covered Product Program

5.1  Commencement

The Dealer’s Covered Product obligations begin on the Production Commencement Date stated in the Activation Certificate. The Production Commencement Date should allow a reasonable implementation period following Actual Funding.

5.2  Exclusive Routing of Covered Products Sold

During the Program Term, the Dealer will route 100% of the Covered Products actually sold in Eligible Retail Transactions through the Sponsor Platform, except for an approved exclusion.

5.3  Eligible Retail Transaction

An “Eligible Retail Transaction” means a retail vehicle transaction that is completed and delivered; is recorded in the Dealer’s DMS; is eligible for the applicable Covered Product; occurs in a jurisdiction where the product may lawfully be offered; and is not otherwise excluded in Addendum A.

Eligible Retail Transactions exclude wholesale transactions; dealer trades; unwinds; rescinded transactions; transactions in which the customer declines the product; transactions in which the product is legally unavailable; transactions rejected by the Sponsor for a legitimate documented reason; and other exclusions stated in Addendum A.

5.4  Compliant Offer Standard

The Dealer will cause its properly trained personnel to make Covered Products reasonably available for compliant presentation in eligible transactions. This provision does not require a customer to buy a product; misleading bundling; a hidden charge; a false representation that a product is mandatory; the falsification of a customer election; improper packing of payments; or conduct prohibited by law.

5.5  No Diversion

Without prior written approval, the Dealer may not:

  1. Route a Covered Product to another provider;
  2. Replace the Sponsor;
  3. Divert Covered Product proceeds;
  4. Redirect remittances;
  5. Move production to an affiliate to avoid this Agreement;
  6. Conceal Covered Product production;
  7. Reclassify a Covered Product to avoid reporting;
  8. Establish a substantially similar avoidance arrangement; or
  9. Interfere with an approved controlled account or proceeds assignment.

5.6  Products Not Available Through Sponsor

A product is not subject to exclusivity when the Sponsor is not legally authorized to provide it; the Sponsor does not offer it; the Sponsor cannot timely process it; the Sponsor rejects an otherwise eligible contract without reasonable grounds; a regulator prohibits the product; or the Parties approve an exception in writing.

5.7  Exact Production Thresholds

The Dealer must satisfy the exact production, penetration, routing, gross-production, remittance, and reporting requirements in Addendum A. No production threshold may be enforced unless the relevant baseline and exact operative number appear in the completed Addendum A.

8 Remittance and Repayment Support

8.1  Approved Remittance Structure

The Dealer will follow the remittance, lockbox, controlled-account, proceeds-assignment, or ACH structure stated in the Funding Documents and Addendum A.

8.2  Minimum Coverage

Unless a higher amount is stated in Addendum A, monthly Eligible Net Remittance must equal or exceed the greater of:

  1. The fixed minimum stated in Addendum A; or
  2. 125% of the scheduled monthly principal, interest, recurring fees, and other scheduled debt service under the Funding Facility.

8.3  ACH True-Up

If Eligible Net Remittance is insufficient to satisfy a scheduled payment, the Dealer must pay the shortage through ACH or another approved source within the cure period stated in Addendum A. An ACH true-up cures the payment shortage but does not automatically cure product diversion; falsified reporting; repeated production failure; unauthorized provider replacement; or interference with collateral or account control.

8.4  Protected Funds

8.5  Excess Remittance

Excess Eligible Net Remittance will be handled as stated in the Funding Documents and may be applied to scheduled repayment; principal reduction; reserve buildup; reconciliation; approved release to the Dealer; or another expressly agreed purpose.

11 Production Deficiencies, Defaults, and Remedies

11.1  Production Deficiency

A “Production Deficiency” occurs when the Dealer fails an ordinary monthly production, penetration, reporting, or remittance test but the failure has not yet become a Program Default.

11.2  Deficiency Notice

Following a Production Deficiency:

  1. Qualified Commercial or the Sponsor will provide written notice;
  2. The Dealer will provide a corrective-action plan within five business days;
  3. A payment shortage must be cured within the period stated in Addendum A;
  4. Additional monitoring may be imposed;
  5. Undrawn advances or holdbacks may be suspended by the party legally controlling them; and
  6. The Parties will conduct a production and capital-health review.

11.3  Repeated Production Default

Unless Addendum A states a different objective test, an ordinary production deficiency becomes a “Program Default” when the same material monthly covenant is missed for two consecutive months; a material monthly covenant is missed in three months of a rolling six-month period; a rolling three-month covenant is missed; a remittance shortage remains uncured; or the Dealer fails to implement an agreed corrective-action plan.

11.4  Immediate Program Default

The following may constitute an immediate Program Default without an ordinary production cure period:

  1. Intentional product diversion;
  2. Intentional remittance diversion;
  3. Fraud;
  4. Material falsification of production or financial reporting;
  5. Unauthorized replacement of the Sponsor;
  6. Misappropriation of controlled funds;
  7. Intentional interference with account control;
  8. Forgery or unauthorized signature;
  9. Material misuse of funding proceeds;
  10. Concealment of a material ownership change;
  11. Intentional obstruction of a legitimate audit;
  12. Loss of the Dealer’s license causing operations to cease; or
  13. Another immediate default expressly identified in the Funding Documents.

11.5  Sponsor-Caused Shortfall

The Dealer will not be deemed in default to the extent a shortfall was directly caused by Sponsor platform failure; Sponsor refusal to process otherwise eligible contracts; Sponsor loss of authority; Sponsor failure to provide an agreed product; Sponsor reporting error; Sponsor material breach; a government prohibition affecting the product; or another event outside the Dealer’s reasonable control that the Dealer could not reasonably mitigate.

11.6  Force Majeure and Market Conditions

Temporary production deterioration caused by a documented disaster, government closure, material manufacturer interruption, regional market disruption, or comparable event will be addressed through a good-faith corrective plan rather than automatic acceleration, unless the Dealer is also in uncured payment default.

11.7  Available Remedies

Subject to applicable law and the Funding Documents, remedies may include additional reporting; a corrective-action plan; suspension of undrawn availability; suspension of holdback releases; increased reserves; cash trapping; ACH true-up; additional collateral requests; replacement of deficient account-control arrangements; termination of preferential program economics; a specifically disclosed prospective pricing adjustment; enforcement of proceeds assignments; termination of this Agreement; and Funding Party remedies under the Funding Documents.

11.8  Rate Adjustments

No rate increase may be imposed merely by referring to a “standard” or “non-subsidized” rate. Any pricing adjustment must be:

  1. Specifically stated in the Funding Documents or Activation Certificate;
  2. Expressed as an exact rate or number of basis points;
  3. Prospective;
  4. Triggered by an objective event;
  5. Subject to applicable cure rights;
  6. Lawful; and
  7. Imposed only by a party authorized under the Funding Documents.

If the applicable field in the Activation Certificate is blank, no program-related rate adjustment applies.

11.9  Acceleration

Qualified Commercial or the Sponsor may not accelerate the Funding Facility unless it is the Funding Party or has express written enforcement authority. Acceleration is governed by the Funding Documents.

11.10  No Undisclosed Penalty

No Party may impose an undisclosed penalty, speculative lost-profit charge, or punitive fee for a production deficiency.

11.11  Restoration

After an ordinary Program Default is cured and the Dealer satisfies the applicable requirements for three consecutive months, suspended program benefits may be restored prospectively as agreed by the authorized Parties.

12 Sponsor or Program Manager Default

12.1  Sponsor Default

A Sponsor default may occur if the Sponsor loses required authority; materially fails to administer Covered Products; materially fails to remit required funds; repeatedly rejects eligible contracts without justification; commits fraud; materially breaches data-security duties; provides materially false reports; or otherwise materially breaches this Agreement.

12.2  Program Manager Default

A Program Manager default may occur if Qualified Commercial knowingly makes unauthorized lending promises; materially misuses Dealer information; conceals material compensation; materially breaches the Capital Health Services obligations; commits fraud; or otherwise materially breaches this Agreement.

12.3  Cure

Except for fraud, intentional misuse of funds, or a material security incident requiring immediate action, the defaulting Party will have ten business days after notice to begin a cure and thirty days to complete it when cure reasonably requires additional time.

12.4  Alternative Product Provider

If the Sponsor cannot lawfully or operationally continue, the Dealer may use an alternate provider approved by Qualified Commercial and, where required, the Funding Party. The Dealer will not be treated as diverting production while an approved replacement is being implemented.

12.5  Funding Obligations Continue

A Sponsor or Program Manager default does not eliminate the Dealer’s payment obligations to the Funding Party.

13 Protected Relationships and Non-Circumvention

13.1  Activation Required

This Section 13 does not apply unless Actual Funding occurred.

13.2  Protected Transactions

During the Program Term and for twelve months after it ends, the Dealer will not knowingly bypass Qualified Commercial in connection with a renewal of the funded facility; an increase with the same Protected Funding Source; a refinance by the same Protected Funding Source; a substantially related facility arising from Qualified Commercial’s funded introduction; or a transaction intentionally restructured to avoid Qualified Commercial’s disclosed rights.

13.3  Limitations

This Section does not apply to a preexisting relationship disclosed in Schedule 4; a publicly available lender relationship not introduced through Qualified Commercial; a lender that independently contacted the Dealer without use of Qualified Commercial confidential information; consumer auto financing; ordinary floorplan credit disclosed in Schedule 4; financing unrelated to the funded program; a source Qualified Commercial declined or failed to pursue within a reasonable period; or financing closed after Qualified Commercial provided written consent.

13.4  No Ownership of Dealer Relationships

Qualified Commercial does not own the Dealer, the Dealer’s customers, or all of the Dealer’s financing relationships. This Section protects only specifically identified funded or materially introduced relationships.

15 Privacy, Information Security, and Customer Data

15.1  Limited Purpose

Each Party receiving protected information will maintain safeguards reasonably appropriate to the information, including as applicable role-based access; least-privilege access; multi-factor authentication; encryption in transit and at rest; secure document transmission; logging; device security; credential management; secure disposal; incident-response procedures; vendor oversight; and workforce training.

To the extent Qualified Commercial or the Sponsor acts as a service provider with access to customer information, it will:

  1. Use the information only to provide authorized services;
  2. Maintain appropriate safeguards;
  3. Limit subcontractor access;
  4. Contractually require appropriate subcontractor protections;
  5. Cooperate with reasonable security assessments;
  6. Notify the Dealer of a material security incident;
  7. Assist with legally required response and notification; and
  8. Return or securely dispose of information when no longer required.

15.5  Security Incident Notice

A Party discovering unauthorized access to protected information will notify affected Parties without unreasonable delay and, contractually, within 24 hours after confirming a material incident, unless prohibited by law or law enforcement. The notice will provide available information concerning the nature of the incident; information affected; systems involved; containment; remediation; required notices; and ongoing risk.

15.6  No Unauthorized Marketing

Dealer Customer Information may not be used for unrelated marketing, lead generation, data resale, or independent solicitation without a lawful basis and required notices or consents.

15.7  Separate Data Addendum

The Parties will execute a separate information-security or data-processing addendum when required by the Funding Party, Sponsor Platform, applicable law, or the nature of system access.
