10 Year Term Loan Program
Commercial working capital and high-interest business debt refinancing | Partner and borrower
eligibility guide
Loan amount
$50,000 to $350,000
Repayment term
10 years / 120 months
Target interest rate
14%–20%; may be higher depending on risk profile
Estimated closing
2–4 weeks, subject to underwriting and documentation
Program fee
5.5% of approved loan amount, deducted from loan proceeds
Availability
All 50 U.S. states; Puerto Rico excluded
1 Eligible uses of funds
• Working capital: General operating liquidity and business working capital needs.
• Refinancing: Payoff of eligible high-interest business debt, including merchant cash advances (MCAs).
• Mandatory MCA payoff: Any active MCAs must be refinanced as part of the new loan; up to 2 MCAs and up to 7 total
debts may be refinanced.
• Not permitted: Business acquisitions; real estate development, real estate investment, or other real estate-sector
businesses.
2 Minimum borrower requirements
Business history
At least 2 years; 4 years for construction businesses.
Revenue
At least $175,000 annual revenue on the most recently filed business tax
return.
Profitability
Positive EBITDA on the most recently filed business tax return.
Credit
Minimum 680 Equifax FICO Score 9 for every 20%+ beneficial owner; hard
minimum.
Credit inquiry
Soft credit pull only; eligible to recheck after 30 days.
Ownership / residency
All guarantors must be U.S. citizens or lawful permanent residents; at least
51% of ownership must be held by U.S. citizens or lawful permanent residents.
Business operations
U.S.-based company operations and owners; majority of income must be
domestic.

3 Loan sizing pathways
Fast Track | up to $200,000
40% of annual tax-return revenue minus current business debt, capped at
$200,000. No DSCR test. Maximum third-position blanket business-assets UCC.
Cash-flow / DSCR | up to $350,000
Lesser of (a) 40% of annual tax-return revenue or (b) 60% of annual tax-return
revenue minus current business debt, capped at $350,000. Minimum 1.10x
DSCR. Maximum fourth-position blanket business-assets UCC.
Loan sizing is indicative; final loan amount depends on lender underwriting, debt payoffs, and other eligibility requirements.
4 Bank statement requirements | trailing six months
NSF activity
No more than 5 non-sufficient-funds / overdraft items.
Revenue consistency
Annualized qualifying bank deposits must not reflect more than a 25% revenue
decline compared with the most recently filed business tax return.
Liquidity
Average ending bank balance must exceed 3 times the proposed monthly loan
payment.
5 Security, UCC and guarantees
• Blanket UCC lien on all business assets is required; maximum position depends on the loan-sizing pathway.
• Equipment-specific UCC filings do not count as all-business-assets blanket UCC positions for this screening rule.
• Personal guarantees are required from every 20%+ owner. No separately pledged personal collateral is required.
6 Risk and exclusion criteria
Criminal history
No financial-crime-related felony conviction for any guarantor; no felony
conviction within the last 3 years.
Bankruptcy
All historical bankruptcies discharged; no bankruptcy filed within the last 5
years.
IRS balances
Aggregate IRS balances must not exceed $100,000.
Judgments and liens
No active judgments or liens within the past 5 years, subject to program
review.
Litigation
No active collection-related litigation and none filed within the last 3 years.
Other active litigation is reviewed case by case.
7 Submission screening and closing
• Confirm eligible industry, use of funds, geographic location, time in business, domestic revenue, ownership and
credit.

Program notes
This guide consolidates the September 2026 NEWITY Growth Term Loan criteria, October 21, 2026 partner briefing excerpts, and
supplemental operational guidelines supplied for Quidity Direct. Some operational details (including the Equifax model, soft-pull policy,
30-day recheck and closing timeline) are from supplemental guidance rather than the September criteria sheet. All terms are subject to
lender confirmation and approval. NEWITY LLC and its affiliates act as lender service providers, not lenders.
